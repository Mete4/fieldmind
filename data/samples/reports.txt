## 2 | Battalion 3
Battalion 3 to Command. Spot fires are landing on Pentz Road near Feather River Hospital. Hospital staff have started moving patients, they're estimating sixty patients to transport. We need every available ambulance and buses staged on Pentz Road, and an engine company to protect the hospital.

## 3 | Medic 41
Medic 41 to Command, at Paradise Community Park. One patient, elderly male, chest pain and heavy smoke inhalation, conscious and breathing, we're treating and transporting. Shelter crowd here is calm, about forty people waiting for buses.

## 4 | Truck 82
Truck 82 to Command, urgent traffic. Partial roof collapse on a two-story commercial building at Clark Road and Elliott Road. We can hear three people inside calling for help. Need technical rescue, the heavy rescue unit, and a medic unit staged. Fire has not reached this block yet.

## 5 | Engine 83

## 6 | Paradise PD 7
PD 7 to Command. Skyway at Neal Road is blocked, abandoned vehicles in both northbound lanes and evacuation traffic is backed up to Pearson Road. No injuries. Need two tow trucks and a traffic control unit here, and consider contra-flow on Skyway.

## 7 | Dispatch

## 8 | Engine 35
Engine 35 to Command. Wind-driven vegetation fire at Wagstaff Road and Clark Road, running north with the wind, three structures immediately threatened. We're on the first structure with one line. Need two more engines and a water tender, hydrant pressure is dropping.

## 9 | Station 26
Station 26 to Command. Fire is in the canyon below Centerville and has cut Centerville Road at the bridge. Six homes above the road are cut off, we estimate fifteen people, no injuries reported, but they cannot get out toward the Skyway. Need an escort and a water drop on the road.

## 10 | Medic 42
